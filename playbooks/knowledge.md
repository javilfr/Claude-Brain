# Knowledge

Generated from `knowledge.jsonl` by the learning pipeline. Do not edit by hand.

## Accuracy

- Keep one canonical facts file per project (dates, names, numbers and counts the owner stated) and check every derived file against it by script. The owner corrects wrong dates and counts quickly; this stops them recurring. `know-facts-file`

## Audit

- When the owner pastes a list of required deliverables, answer with a table of present, missing and wrong for each item, then fix what can be fixed. If the repo is empty, the first sentence says so. `know-rubric-table`

## Legal

- Every claim of law carries its source: the statute article, regulation or ruling, with its date. Separate settled law from interpretation and state the limits of the analysis. `know-law-sources`

## Long writing

- Write long pieces in batches of at most about 1,500 words per write, and report each part's measured word count against its target in a table. Large single writes timed out in earlier projects. `know-batches`
- For book-length work, keep an outline plus per-chapter status as the state, and load one chapter at a time, never the whole manuscript. `know-chapter-state`
- When the owner states a length (600 words each, 800 to 1,000 per article), meet it and show a script count per item. He reads length as a sign of thoroughness in plans and briefs. `know-stated-lengths`

## Research

- Check citations in two steps: the link resolves, and a fresh reader confirms the cited passage supports the sentence. Fabricated or mismatched citations remain a live failure for current models. `know-cite-check`
- Tag each factual claim in a research deliverable with its source (URL, or file and line) or mark it as an estimate. An untagged claim cannot be checked later. `know-claim-tags`
- In research and strategy deliverables, state the strongest contrary evidence and the unknowns rather than burying them; readers act on these documents without re-checking. `know-contrary-evidence`
- Prefer primary sources and official documentation; flag secondary summaries as secondary and give each source's date. `know-primary-sources`
- Search existing research memos and memory before researching again, and save each research result as a memo a later session can reuse. `know-reuse-memos`

## Verify

- Run a link check over every document deliverable, internal anchors included, before delivery. `know-links-resolve`
