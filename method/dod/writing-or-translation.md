# Definition of done: Writing or translation

Read before delivering written or translated content.

- [ ] Does it meet the brief: audience, purpose, voice, and length measured by script per part?
- [ ] Were facts, names, dates and numbers checked against the sources or the project's facts file?
- [ ] Was it spell-checked in the declared language and locale?
- [ ] Is it free of stock phrasing: rule-of-three padding, summaries that restate the body, hedging stacks?
- [ ] For translation: are segment counts, placeholders, numbers and dates the same as the source?
- [ ] For translation: was a sample back-translated blind and compared, and read once for naturalness?
- [ ] Do terms match the glossary, with names left untranslated unless listed?
- [ ] Is it in the language the owner asked for?
