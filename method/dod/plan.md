# Definition of done: Plan

Read before presenting a plan or strategy.

- [ ] Does every ledger line map to a part of the plan?
- [ ] Does each item or phase have an exit check that someone can observe?
- [ ] Are assumptions listed and marked, each recorded in `.brain/DECISIONS.md`?
- [ ] Is the plan sized to the request, with nothing built ahead of need?
- [ ] Are irreversible steps flagged, with the top risks and their mitigations?
- [ ] Are impossible or unverifiable parts of the request reframed in plain words, up front?
- [ ] Do estimates say what they rest on (measured, analogous or guessed)?
- [ ] Is the first step unambiguous, with nothing depending on an unanswered fork-level question?
