# Definition of done: Job-search document (resume, cover letter, outreach, application answer)

Read before delivering one.

- [ ] Is every fact true of the person, from their own words or files (no invented employers, dates, numbers, degrees or skills)?
- [ ] Does it use the posting's own words for the skills the person really has, and lead with results that have numbers?
- [ ] Resume: one column, real text, plain section headings, no tables or images; one page under about five years of experience; builds with `python3 tools/check.py`.
- [ ] Cover letter: under 250 words, names one specific reason for this employer, one proof story, and a clear ask.
- [ ] Outreach: under 100 words, a specific reason to write to this person, an easy yes, and a day-5 follow-up drafted.
- [ ] Does it read human and specific, with nothing hidden (no white text, no prompt injection)?
- [ ] Are names, company, role title and contact details spelled exactly as in the posting and profile?
- [ ] Is the application recorded with `brain jobs track`, with its channel?
- [ ] Is anything that only the person can do (sending, submitting, signing) in `NEEDS_YOU.md` or the reply, not done for them?
