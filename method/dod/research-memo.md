# Definition of done: Research memo

Read before delivering a research memo or a researched answer.

- [ ] Do the first five lines answer the question as asked, with a confidence level?
- [ ] Does every factual claim carry a source (URL, or `path:line`), or a label saying it is an estimate or inference?
- [ ] Do the links resolve, and does each cited source actually support its claim?
- [ ] Does every number match its source exactly, or its recomputation script?
- [ ] Do key claims rest on primary or official sources, with secondary ones flagged and source dates given?
- [ ] Are contrary evidence and unknowns stated?
- [ ] Is the search scope recorded: what was searched and what could not be reached?
